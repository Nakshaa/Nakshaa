## Keshab Thapa

Originally from Nepal, I am based in Winnipeg, Canada. I have provided GIS and remote sensing support to Indigenous Nations in Manitoba and Ontario. I use Google Earth Engine, open-source Python packages such as geemap and Leafmap, GeoLibre, QGIS, Copernicus platforms, NASA platforms, and ArcGIS Pro and ArcGIS Online in my GIS mapping projects. My learning journey involves analyzing satellite imagery through remote sensing using open-source software such as GeoLibre, Google Earth Engine, Geemap, Leafmap, etc., and communicating information for land protection. I am keen on designing open-source packages that an eighth-grader can use to map their land and share their maps.
Some of my published work can be accessed [here](https://naksaa.wordpress.com/home/).
